Frequently Asked Questions

Is Shadow313 PIPEDA compliant?

What is the 313-BIND protocol?

313-BIND is Shadow313's proprietary cryptographic audit receipt system — nanosecond timestamps ending in ...313, SLH-DSA (FIPS 205) signatures, SHA3-512 chain hashing, and optional IPFS anchoring for tamper-evident, independently verifiable audit trails.
