313-BIND Temporal Binding Protocol — Receipt Chain
STEP 1
Payload Input
Security finding, audit event, or module output dict
→
STEP 2
Timestamp ...313
time.time_ns() nudged to end in ...313 (~1ms entropy)
→
STEP 3
SHA3-256 Hash
payload_hash = sha3_256(canonical_json)
→
STEP 4
SHA3-512 Chain
chain_hash = sha3_512(payload_hash + prev_hash + ts)
→
STEP 5
SLH-DSA Sign
FIPS 205 signature over chain_hash (pyspx or HMAC fallback)
→
STEP 6
BindReceipt
Immutable receipt: bind_id, ts_ns, hashes, signature
→
STEP 7
IPFS Anchor
Optional: CIDv1 anchor for external verification
→
VERIFY
Gap Detection
