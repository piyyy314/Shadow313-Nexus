Privacy Policy

Shadow313 NEXUS is designed from the ground up to be local-first and privacy-preserving. We collect zero PII from CLI users.

Last updated: October 2026  ·  Jurisdiction: Ottawa, ON, Canada (PIPEDA)

What We Collect

CLI Tool — Zero Collection

The Shadow313 CLI collects nothing. All processing is local:

  • No telemetry or usage analytics
  • No crash reports sent externally
  • No API keys or credentials transmitted
  • All AI inference runs locally via Ollama
  • 313-BIND receipts are stored locally only
  • IPFS anchoring is opt-in and uses public data only

Web Properties — Minimal

Our websites (shadow313.dev, shadow313.com) may collect:

  • Standard web server access logs (IP, timestamp, path) — retained 30 days
  • Contact form submissions (name, email, message) — used only to respond
  • No tracking cookies or advertising pixels
  • No third-party analytics (no Google Analytics, no Mixpanel)
  • No fingerprinting or behavioral tracking

Your Rights (PIPEDA)

Under Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), you have the right to:

  • Access — Request a copy of any personal information we hold about you
  • Correction — Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Withdrawal — Withdraw consent for processing at any time
  • Complaint — File a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada
