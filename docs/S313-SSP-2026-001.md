### 313-BIND Temporal Binding Protocol

The 313-BIND protocol provides AU-9 (Protection of Audit Info) and AU-10 (Non-repudiation) at a level that **exceeds** NIST SP 800-53 baseline requirements:

- **Timestamp entropy:** Nanosecond timestamps ending in `...313` (unforgeable without controlling the system clock)
- **Signature:** SLH-DSA-SHAKE-128f (FIPS 205) — quantum-resistant, 17,088-byte signatures
- **Chain integrity:** SHA3-512 Merkle chain — each receipt links to the previous
- **IPFS anchoring:** SHA3-256 CIDv1 multihash — quantum-resistant content addressing
- **Fallback chain:** pyspx (pure-Python) → HMAC-SHA3-256 (air-gapped deployments)
