Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data ever sent to the cloud?

No. Shadow313 is local-first by design. All processing happens on your machine. The only optional cloud component is IPFS anchoring for 313-BIND receipts — and that only uploads cryptographic hashes (SHA3-512), never your actual security data or findings.

What AI models are supported?

Any Ollama-compatible model runs locally. We recommend llama3.2:3b, mistral:7b, and codellama:7b. No OpenAI API key required. No data leaves your network. The Shadow313-Nexus custom model is available at ollama.com/matarmohamad313/shadow313-nexus.

Can I use Shadow313 in an air-gapped environment?

Yes. The Enterprise tier is specifically designed for air-gapped deployments. All dependencies can be pre-downloaded and the platform runs fully offline. We provide an offline bundle with all required models and dependencies.

What is the 313-BIND temporal binding protocol?

313-BIND creates cryptographically verifiable, tamper-evident audit receipts for every security finding. Each receipt includes a nanosecond timestamp ending in ...313, an SLH-DSA (FIPS 205) signature, and an optional IPFS anchor. This makes your security posture provable to auditors without trusting any central authority.

Is there a free trial for Professional?

Yes — 14-day free trial, no credit card required. Contact us at matarmohamad313@duck.com to get started.

What does "post-quantum" mean for my security?

Shadow313 uses NIST FIPS 203/204/205 algorithms (ML-KEM-768, ML-DSA-65, SLH-DSA) that are resistant to attacks from cryptographically relevant quantum computers (CRQCs). RSA and ECDSA are deprecated throughout the platform. Your audit receipts will remain verifiable even after quantum computers become available.
